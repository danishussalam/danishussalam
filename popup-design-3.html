APE

Action, Purpose, Expectation

What It Does

APE emphasizes actionable outcomes. Specify what action you want, why it matters (purpose), and what success looks like.

Best For

Project work, process optimization, and deliverable-focused tasks.

Goal-Oriented

When to Use

Use APE when you need clear, measurable results. Perfect for business tasks, development sprints, and any project where outcomes are quantifiable.

Example

"As a product manager, I need to write a feature proposal that justifies why this feature improves user retention, formatted as a 2-page document."
