Frequently Asked Questions

What is VoiceAI?
VoiceAI is a voice-first interview simulator built specifically for accounting professionals. It uses AI to ask realistic questions, listen to your voice answers, and provide instant feedback on how well you communicate — clarity, structure, tone, technical depth, and confidence.
How does the voice recording work?
We use your browser's built-in speech recognition (free, no app needed). Click the mic button, speak your answer, and it automatically converts to text. Your answer goes to our AI for evaluation. No recordings are stored — only the text and feedback.
How is my answer scored?
Our AI evaluates your response across five dimensions: Clarity (how easy it is to follow), Structure (logical flow), Professional Tone (confidence and vocabulary), Technical Depth (accounting knowledge), and Confidence (absence of hesitation). Each gets a 0–10 score, and we average them for your overall score.
Is my voice/data private?
Yes. Your audio is never recorded or stored. We only see the text transcript that your browser converts from your voice. Your interview answers and feedback are kept private — we don't sell data or train models on your content.
Is VoiceAI free?
VoiceAI is in beta and currently free for all users. We're gathering feedback to improve the product. Future pricing (if any) will be announced clearly — no surprise charges.
How is VoiceAI different from other interview tools?
Most interview prep tools focus on language learning or offer generic feedback. VoiceAI is built by accounting professionals, for accountants. We ask role-specific questions, evaluate communication skills (not English proficiency), and give actionable feedback in seconds. No scripts, no templates—just you, a real interviewer, and honest feedback.
